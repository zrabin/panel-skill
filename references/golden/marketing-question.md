# Council Question: Activation Channel for B2B SaaS Free Trial

Should we prioritize an in-app onboarding checklist over a human-led
onboarding call as the primary activation lever for new free-trial signups,
given that our ACV is $4,800/year, our current trial-to-paid conversion rate
is 11%, our support team can handle at most 30 calls per week, and our
primary user persona is a solo operations manager at a 20–200 person company?
